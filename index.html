Background

BG Renewables was founded in 2010 with a core philosophy to enable social and environmental change.

Utilising over 25 years global development and delivery experience in renewables, telecoms and oil and gas projects, including senior management in construction of major wind developments, BG Renewables provides a broad perspective of the energy transition landscape.

Alongside BG Renewables, Ben Gowers (founder) is co-founder of Morwind and Channel Gateway and holds non executive roles in sustainable, clean energy and community companies.

Services

BG Renewables has an extensive track record across onshore wind, offshore wind, solar and marine, as well as working more widely on sustainable, clean and low carbon solutions. With a fundamental understanding of the key market drivers and regulatory demands, we support established and emerging companies in the renewable and marine industries.

Ventures

Alongside advisory work, Ben has co-founded two ventures that put this philosophy into practice, focused on developing both offshore wind projects and its supply chain to enable long term social, economic and environmental benefit.

Morwind

A specialist wind developer based in the South West of England. Morwind is working to develop the region’s offshore wind resource sustainably, including floating offshore wind, for the benefit of enabling economic, social and environmental change to the region and the wider UK.

Visit morwind.co.uk

Channel Gateway

A proposed £500 million clean energy port facility next to Portland Port in Dorset. It would provide a deepwater construction and assembly platform for fixed and floating wind turbine foundations, supporting offshore wind in the English Channel, the Celtic Sea and beyond, and aims to bring high-quality jobs and investment to the local area. Feasibility work is backed by The Crown Estate and Dorset Council, and the project is part of the Dorset Clean Energy Super Cluster.

Visit channelgateway.co.uk
